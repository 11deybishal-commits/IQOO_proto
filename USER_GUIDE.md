# SentinelOps - Complete User Guidance & Instruction File

Welcome to the **SentinelOps** platform, an Enterprise-Grade AI-Powered Incident Intelligence Platform. This guide is designed to provide you with complete instructions on how to use the platform, explaining what buttons and information you will see throughout the application.

---

## 1. Authentication (Login / Register)
When you first access the application, you will be directed to the Authentication page.
- **Login Tab**: Enter your email and password if you already have an account. Click the **"Login"** button to enter the dashboard.
- **Register Tab**: If you do not have an account, switch to this tab. Fill in your full name, email, and password, then click the **"Register"** button to create your account.

---

## 2. Global Navigation & Top Bar
Once logged in, the Top Bar is always visible at the top of the screen.
- **Command Palette (🔍 Search)**: Press `Ctrl + K` (or `⌘ + K` on Mac) anywhere in the app to quickly search across all incidents. You can also click the Search bar in the top navigation. 
- **Notifications Panel (🔔 Bell Icon)**: Click the bell icon to view real-time incident alerts. You can click on notifications to view the incident details or use the "Dismiss" action to clear them.
- **User Profile Menu (👤 Avatar)**: Located on the top right. Click this to access a dropdown menu where you can navigate to **Settings** or **Log out** of the platform.

---

## 3. Dashboard (`/dashboard`)
The Dashboard is your main overview of platform activity.
- **Live Stats Cards**: At the top, you will see high-level statistics like total incidents, active incidents, and resolved metrics. 
- **Recent Incidents Table**: Displays a list of the most recent incidents. Clicking on a row will typically take you to the detailed view of that incident.
- **Activity Feed**: Shows a timeline of recent events, such as when an incident was created, updated, or when the AI finished its analysis.

---

## 4. Incidents Management (`/incidents`)
This page allows you to view, filter, and manage all incidents.
- **"Create Incident" Button**: Click this button (usually at the top right) to report a new incident. 
  - **Create Incident Form**: You will need to provide a **Title**, **Description**, and **Severity** (e.g., Low, Medium, High, Critical).
  - **Submit**: Once submitted, SentinelOps automatically triggers a background AI analysis pipeline to investigate.
- **Incident Status & Severity Indicators**: The table displays colorful badges indicating the current status (e.g., `Investigating`, `Resolved`) and severity.
- **Search & Filters**: Use the text search or dropdown filters at the top of the list to narrow down incidents by their status or severity.

### Incident Detail View
When you click on an incident from the list:
- **Details Section**: Shows the title, description, time created, and reporter information.
- **AI Analysis Panel**: This is the core of SentinelOps. It displays:
  - The comprehensive **Root Cause Analysis** generated by the AI (LLaMa 3.3 70B).
  - **Actionable Recommendations** for resolving the incident.
- **Status Update Buttons**: You can manually update the status of the incident (e.g., mark as "Resolved" or "Closed") once the recommendations have been applied.

---

## 5. Knowledge Base (`/knowledge`)
This section allows you to feed historical data and standard operating procedures (SOPs) into the AI's memory.
- **"Upload Document" Button**: Click this to open a file uploader. You can upload runbooks, SOPs, or log files. These documents are indexed using FAISS vector search, making the AI smarter for future incidents.
- **Document List**: Shows previously uploaded files. You can click on them to view details or manage them.

---

## 6. Settings (`/settings`)
Manage your account and platform preferences.
- **Profile Management**: Update your name, email, or change your password.
- **Security Overview**: View active sessions or update security-related configurations.
- **Notifications Config**: Toggle how you receive alerts (e.g., in-app, email).
- **Integrations Status**: Check the status of backend connections, such as the Groq AI integration or database connectivity.

---

## 💡 Keyboard Shortcuts
- `Ctrl + K` (or `⌘ + K`): Open global search (Command Palette).
- `Escape (Esc)`: Close any open dialogs, modals, or the command palette.

## 🤖 How the AI Pipeline Works in the Background
1. **Incident Creation**: You click "Submit" on a new incident.
2. **Auto-Status**: The incident instantly enters the `Investigating` status.
3. **Retrieval**: The AI looks up historical incidents and uploaded Knowledge Base docs using Vector Search.
4. **Diagnosis**: The LLM analyzes the data and produces a final diagnostic report.
5. **Results**: The incident page automatically updates with the AI's root cause analysis and recommendations!
