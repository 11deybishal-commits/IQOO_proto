# SentinelOps - Complete User Guidance & Instruction File

Welcome to the **SentinelOps** platform, an Enterprise-Grade AI-Powered Incident Intelligence Platform. This guide provides complete instructions on how to use the platform, including every feature, section, button, and real-time capability available.

---

## 1. Authentication (Login / Register)
When you first access the application, you will be directed to the Authentication page.
- **Login Tab**: Enter your email and password if you already have an account. Click the **"Login"** button to enter the dashboard.
- **Register Tab**: If you do not have an account, switch to this tab. Fill in your full name, email, and password, then click the **"Register"** button to create your account.

---

## 2. Global Navigation & Top Bar
The Top Bar is always visible across the platform for quick actions.
- **Command Palette (🔍 Search)**: Press `Ctrl + K` (or `⌘ + K` on Mac) anywhere in the app to quickly search across all incidents and platform pages. You can also click the Search bar in the top navigation.
- **Notifications Panel (🔔 Bell Icon)**: Click the bell icon to view real-time incident alerts. Click on notifications to view the incident details or use the "Dismiss" action to clear them.
- **Voice Command (🎤 Mic Icon)**: Located in the top bar, click the mic icon to activate voice-controlled AI chatops. You can speak commands directly to the SentinelOps AI to query incidents or trigger actions.
- **User Profile Menu (👤 Avatar)**: Click this to access a dropdown menu where you can navigate to **Settings** or **Log out** of the platform.

---

## 3. Dashboard (`/dashboard`)
The Dashboard is your main overview of platform activity and infrastructure health.
- **Live Stats Cards**: At the top, view high-level statistics like Total Incidents, Active Incidents, and Resolved metrics.
- **Cloud Cost Ticker**: A real-time running ticker that displays estimated cloud resource costs, helping you monitor the financial impact of active infrastructure.
- **Recent Incidents Table**: Displays a list of the most recent incidents. Clicking on a row takes you to the detailed view of that incident.
- **Activity Feed**: Shows a timeline of recent events, such as when an incident was created, updated, or when the AI finished its analysis.

---

## 4. Incidents Management (`/incidents`)
View, filter, and manage all infrastructure incidents.
- **"Create Incident" Button**: Click this button (top right) to report a new incident. 
  - **Create Incident Form**: Provide a **Title**, **Description**, and **Severity** (Low, Medium, High, Critical).
  - **Submit**: Submitting automatically triggers the background AI analysis pipeline to investigate.
- **Incident Status & Severity Indicators**: Badges indicate the current status (e.g., `Investigating`, `Resolved`) and severity.
- **Search & Filters**: Use the text search or dropdown filters at the top of the list to narrow down incidents by status or severity.

### Incident Detail View
- **Details Section**: Shows the title, description, time created, and reporter information.
- **AI Analysis Panel**: Displays the **Root Cause Analysis** and **Actionable Recommendations** generated by the AI.
- **Status Update Buttons**: Manually update the status of the incident (e.g., mark as "Resolved" or "Closed").

---

## 5. ChatOps & AI Assistant (`/chatops`)
Interact with SentinelOps through a conversational interface.
- **Chat Interface**: Type questions about current infrastructure status, specific incidents, or ask for troubleshooting help.
- **"Send" Button**: Submit your query to the LLaMa 3.3-powered AI assistant.
- **Runbook Generation**: The ChatOps assistant can dynamically generate runbooks and execution plans based on your questions.

---

## 6. Blast Radius Analysis (`/blast-radius`)
Visualize the impact of an incident across your entire infrastructure architecture.
- **Topology Graph**: A visual node-based graph showing your services, databases, and network components.
- **Impact Highlighting**: When an incident occurs, the graph highlights the affected node in red and visually traces the "blast radius" (dependent services that are impacted).
- **Node Click**: Click on any node in the graph to see its health status, dependencies, and currently associated incidents.

---

## 7. Self-Healing & Automation (`/self-healing`)
Manage and monitor autonomous remediation actions taken by the platform.
- **Active Executions**: View a list of scripts or automated runbooks currently being executed by the AI to resolve active incidents.
- **"Approve Execution" Button**: For high-risk remediation actions, the AI will pause and require a human operator to click "Approve" before executing the script.
- **Execution Logs**: View real-time terminal output of self-healing scripts as they run.

---

## 8. Incident Forecasting (`/forecasting`)
Predictive analytics for your infrastructure.
- **Predictive Charts**: View time-series graphs that forecast potential resource bottlenecks (e.g., CPU, Memory, Disk Space) before they cause an incident.
- **Anomaly Detection**: Lists detected anomalies in standard metrics patterns, warning you of unusual behavior.
- **"Generate Report" Button**: Export the forecasting data into a readable report for stakeholders.

---

## 9. Knowledge Base (`/knowledge`)
Feed historical data and standard operating procedures (SOPs) into the AI's memory.
- **"Upload Document" Button**: Upload runbooks, SOPs, or log files. These are indexed using FAISS vector search, making the AI smarter.
- **Document List**: Shows previously uploaded files with options to view or delete them.

---

## 10. Settings (`/settings`)
Manage your account and platform preferences.
- **Profile Management**: Update your name, email, or change your password.
- **Security Overview**: View active sessions or update security-related configurations.
- **Notifications Config**: Toggle how you receive alerts (in-app, email).
- **Integrations Status**: Check the status of backend connections, Groq AI integration, and database connectivity.

---

## 💡 Keyboard Shortcuts
- `Ctrl + K` (or `⌘ + K`): Open global search (Command Palette).
- `Escape (Esc)`: Close any open dialogs, modals, or the command palette.

## 🤖 How the AI Pipeline Works in the Background
1. **Incident Creation**: You click "Submit" on a new incident.
2. **Auto-Status**: The incident instantly enters the `Investigating` status.
3. **Retrieval**: The AI looks up historical incidents and uploaded Knowledge Base docs using Vector Search.
4. **Diagnosis**: The LLM analyzes the data, assesses the Blast Radius, and produces a final diagnostic report.
5. **Results & Self-Healing**: The incident page updates with the AI's root cause analysis, and if applicable, queues a Self-Healing action for your approval.
